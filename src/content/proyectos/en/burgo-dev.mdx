## What it is

burgo.dev is the site for Burgo, the name I work under. It presents custom software, the village (my own apps, each at its own address) and how I work, in Spanish and English. It also hosts the eight PDF manuals, one per app, at burgo.dev/manuales.

## How it is built

It is a static Astro site with no UI framework in the browser. The illustrations (the village, the site plan, the services) are SVG drawings made with custom components, with a restrained palette and three typefaces: Newsreader for headlines, Public Sans for text and IBM Plex Mono for labels. It has unit and browser tests, with Playwright and axe-core for accessibility.

## How I publish it

A script builds the site, writes its Caddy file, validates and reloads the configuration, and rolls back on its own if anything fails. A separate test environment lets me check before going to production.

## What it taught me

Explaining calmly and without hype what I do and what I have in production, and keeping a small, fast, accessible site in two languages.
